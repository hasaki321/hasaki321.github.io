I am a first-year MPhil student in Computer Science at the **School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen**, supervised by Prof. Zhizheng Wu. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from **Lanzhou University**.

My research focuses on **simultaneous speech translation**, **speech language models**, and **generative models**. I am particularly interested in building data-efficient systems that translate continuous speech with high quality and low latency.

我是香港中文大学（深圳）数据科学学院计算机科学专业的研究型硕士生，研究方向包括同声传译、语音语言模型和生成式模型。

My recent work **SimulS2ST-Omni** was accepted to the EMNLP 2026 Main Conference. It establishes a state-of-the-art quality-latency frontier among open-source streaming S2TT/S2ST systems using approximately 2,000 hours of paired S2ST data.
